# Travel AI V3 — Decision Canvas

The V3 redesign removes the visible six-step wizard and replaces it with one editable natural-language decision sentence.

## Interaction

`I can leave from [Athens] in [October] for [3 nights] with about [€500 pp] and I want [romantic + food], going as a [couple].`

Each bracketed token opens a local edit tray. The page does not navigate away or expose a long form sequence.

Below the sentence, Trip DNA soft rings explain the request shape:
- Time
- Budget
- Feel / intent
- Effort preference

The CTA stays singular: **Show me my 3**.

## Results

The stable result zone contains exactly three recommendations:
- one large editorial Best Match,
- two alternative cards,
- Fit and Confidence displayed independently,
- factor bars for time, intent, season, effort, budget and evidence,
- compact refinement actions that re-run the Top 3 instead of exposing an endless catalogue.

## Stay layer

After a user chooses a destination, the page reveals the contextual Trip Basket. The STAY lane reads the Linkwise-derived Stay Intelligence graph when that destination has a supply mapping.

No affiliate tracking CTA is rendered from feed presence alone.

## Visual language

Warm mineral / ivory canvas, deep forest and ink, restrained clay accent, editorial serif for destination emotion, neutral sans for evidence and controls, photography-led composition, soft infographic cues, generous negative space and restrained motion.
