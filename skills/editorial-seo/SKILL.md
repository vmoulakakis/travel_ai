# TravelAI Editorial + SEO Skill

Use this skill for destination dossiers, microplace pages, seasonal landing pages and the TravelAI blog.

## Purpose
Publish useful travel intelligence that is current, distinct and internally connected to the TravelAI decision engine.

## Article contract
Default length: ~500 words unless the query requires more.
Each article must anchor itself to a real date/season/opportunity, state who the trip is for, recommend specific places with reasoning, include one logistics note, include season/crowd/value caveats, link to relevant destination or microplace pages, include a concise FAQ when useful, and carry freshness/evidence metadata.

## SEO rules
- no doorway pages
- no mass paraphrase templates
- no invented keyword volumes
- no fake reviews
- no synthetic latest claims without dated evidence
- canonical + hreflang pairing for EL/EN where both exist
- structured data only when the page actually satisfies the schema
- prioritize topical graph quality over raw page count
